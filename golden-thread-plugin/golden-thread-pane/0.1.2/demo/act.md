## Buttons beside the prompt

narration: The gt pane is a column of buttons beside the prompt: the commands you use most, one click each. The list is your own file, and you change it in plain words.
do: Run `bash <scripts>/gt_demo.sh show pane` and show its output as printed: the list seeded from the defaults, a button moved to the top, the list again, and the setting that opens the pane at session start. The demo edits its own copy of the list, never yours.
point: The buttons are yours to arrange, and a change you ask for in words is a change to one small file.
