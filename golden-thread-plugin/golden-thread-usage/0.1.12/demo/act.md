## Know where your allowance stands

narration: Claude Code hands its status line the plan figures on every render. gt-usage records each one, says nothing until you near a limit, and answers "how much have I used?" from what it recorded.
do: Run `bash <scripts>/gt_demo.sh show usage` and show its output as printed: one status-line render at a fixture 82% of the 5-hour window, where the allowance stands from that reading, the reading itself, and the alert lines. The figures are a fixture in the demo's own home, not your plan.
point: The meter is silent until a number matters, and every figure it shows was measured, not guessed.
