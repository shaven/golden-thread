## Hand routine work to your own engine

narration: Routine work, such as summarising a log, can go to an engine on your own hardware instead of Claude. Before a packet leaves, gt scans it, and a credential-shaped line stops it there.
do: Run `bash <scripts>/gt_demo.sh show farm` and show its output as printed: the packet with a credential-shaped line refused (the line and the rule named, never the value), the same packet without it checked and ready with --dry-run, the empty savings ledger of this fresh home, and the ladder setting. Nothing is sent: the demo's engine address has nothing listening.
point: A credential never reaches an engine, local or hosted, and you see what farming saved rather than being told.
